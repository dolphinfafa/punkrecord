# Brand Guidelines Workflow

## Scope
Document identity rules: logo usage, colors, typography, tone, and UI patterns.

## Required Sections
- Brand mission and positioning
- Logo rules (clear space, minimum size, misuse)
- Color system (primary, secondary, neutrals)
- Typography hierarchy
- Voice and messaging principles

## Delivery Standard
- Keep examples practical and implementation-ready.
- Include exact color codes and font fallbacks.
- Use concise language and avoid ambiguity.
