# Internal Communications Workflow

## Output Types
- Announcements
- Status updates
- Decision memos

## Structure
1. Context
2. Decision or update
3. Impact by team
4. Actions and owners
5. Timeline

## Quality Standard
- Use concrete dates and owners.
- Keep messages short and actionable.
- Avoid unclear or open-ended language.
